[h1]» INTRODUCTION[/h1]
Mounted Weapons adds weapon mounts: buildings that hold an ordinary ranged weapon from your stockpile and fire it from a fixed spot, with more range than the same gun in a colonist's hands.

Select a mount, press [b]Choose weapon[/b] and pick a gun from the list of weapons on the map. A hauler carries it over and installs it. The weapon keeps its quality, hit points and art, so a legendary charge rifle on a mount still does legendary damage. [b]Remove weapon[/b] drops it beside the mount, and a destroyed mount drops it too.

A colonist mans the mount from the cell behind it. The gunner's own Shooting skill, sight, manipulation and traits decide the hit chance, and the mount turns part of their misses into hits. Gunners gain Shooting XP for every burst they fire, at the same rate as shooting by hand. The mount stands between the gunner and the enemy, so it is their cover.

With Biotech, a mech subcore or an AI persona core can aim the weapon instead, and the mount needs power rather than a gunner.

[h1]» WHAT IT COVERS[/h1]
Manned mounts, on their own research tab:
[list]
[*][b]Weapon mount[/b] (after Smithing): wood or metal. 115% range, removes 20% of the gunner's misses, light cover.
[*][b]Reinforced weapon mount[/b] (after Machining): 125% range, removes 35% of misses, aims 10% faster, medium cover.
[*][b]Armored gun emplacement[/b] (after Microelectronics): plasteel shield. 135% range, removes 50% of misses, aims 20% faster, heavy cover.
[/list]
Manned mounts fire only within a 120° arc in front of them, so the gunner can't swing the gun round past themselves. The arc shows as an outline when you place or select a mount.

Automated mounts, Biotech only (without Biotech they and their research are not loaded):
[list]
[*][b]Basic auto-mount[/b]: basic subcore, aims like a level 6 shooter, 120% range.
[*][b]Standard auto-mount[/b]: standard subcore, level 10, 125% range, aims 10% faster.
[*][b]High auto-mount[/b]: high subcore, level 14, 130% range, aims 20% faster, accepts attack orders.
[*][b]Persona auto-mount[/b]: AI persona core, level 18, 135% range, aims 30% faster, accepts attack orders.
[/list]
Deconstructing an auto-mount always returns its core. If the mount is destroyed, the core has a 50% chance to be lost.

What can go on a mount:
[list]
[*]Any ranged weapon that fires projectiles or a beam, from vanilla, the DLCs or other mods.
[*]Not single-use launchers (doomsday, triple rocket), grenades, turret guns or biocoded weapons.
[*]The mount ignores pawn-only equip rules, so heavy guns from mods like Vanilla Factions Expanded: Pirates (warcasket guns) and Dead Man's Switch can be mounted.
[*]Weapons with an extra ability, such as the hellcat rifle's burner, can be mounted. The ability can't be used while the weapon is on the mount.
[/list]
A weapon another mount is already waiting for is greyed out in the list, with the name of that mount.

[h1]» SETTINGS[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]General:[/b] firing arc for manned and for automated mounts (30° to unlimited), the most damage a weapon takes when its mount is destroyed, the chance to lose a core, and whether weapon art is centred on the mount.
[*][b]Manned mounts:[/b] range, misses removed and aiming time for each mount.
[*][b]Automated mounts:[/b] range, accuracy and aiming time for each mount (Biotech only).
[/list]
Changes reach mounts already built when you close the window. [b]Reset this tab[/b] puts one tab back to its defaults.

[h1]» COMPATIBILITY[/h1]
[list]
[*]Built for RimWorld 1.6. Requires Harmony. Biotech is supported, not required: it adds the automated mounts.
[*]Safe to add to a running save.
[*]Removing it mid-save deletes every mount, along with any weapon or core still on one. Use [b]Remove weapon[/b] and deconstruct auto-mounts first to get them back.
[*]Combat Extended is not supported.
[*]Two Harmony patches, neither of which skips the original method: the hit chance of manned mounts (ShotReport.HitFactorFromShooter) and passing the mounted weapon to its projectiles (Projectile.Launch).
[/list]

[h1]» FAQ[/h1]
[b]Why does my colonist ignore the raider behind the mount?[/b]
It's outside the 120° arc. Rotate the mount, or widen the arc in the settings.

[b]A chosen weapon isn't being installed.[/b]
Installing is Hauling work, so a colonist with Hauling enabled has to be free. The mount's info panel shows which weapon it's waiting for.

[b]Does a weapon wear out on a mount?[/b]
No. It only takes damage if the mount itself is destroyed.
